# Slack is where work starts; the app is where it is read and signed

**Status:** accepted. Supersedes [ADR 0002](0002-slack-runs-alongside-email-until-reliable.md). The new decision on the Assistant is plan P-56 (2026-11-18): hidden now, deleted once the bar below is met. The Slack demo (plan P-57) follows this boundary: Slack starts and guides each step, the app does upload, redaction review, the Quality Check and every signature.

Slack becomes the main way people find, start and track their work: a Slack app (Socket Mode, so no public address) gives each person an App Home with their Tasks, a DM for each notice, and an `/adj` command for tasks, cases, status and import. Only steps with no signature and no Clinical Content are done fully in Slack: importing or pulling AE records, reassigning a review with a reason, and looking up tasks, cases and status. Every signed step and every step that needs Clinical Content to decide is started in Slack and finished in the app through an *Open in the app* button: the site's upload, review and signature; the Quality Check; adjudication and more-data requests; the committee meeting and the Chair's consensus; re-adjudication; entering an AE by hand. The Case record, document uploads and previews, and measurement stay in the app. Slack advises against asking for passwords "within modals (or any app surface area)" and has no masked input, so no signature is taken in Slack. E-mail continues for every notice and the app keeps every screen, so the workflow never depends on Slack; a failed DM is a recorded Delivery Attempt. Only the Assistant may be retired, after a Slack equivalent of each of its abilities is covered by tests, 3 consecutive full demo runs are driven from Slack, and `tools.notify_parity` is clean on each, and then only by a new decision; the other screens are permanent. Pharma users live on Teams, so the same boundary applies to a Teams app later.

## Consequences

- In the demo, one Slack account is Acting As every person through `/adj act-as`, simulated as the app's sidebar is. Real use maps each Slack user to one person in `users.yaml`.
- Every Slack action is written to a hash-chained `data/slack/actions.jsonl` with the Slack user ID; case events stay as they would be from the app.
